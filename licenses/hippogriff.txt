heff began as a port of the Hippogriff reference implementation
(https://tangled.org/owenlynch.tngl.sh/hippogriff, at ed6c607), and
parts of it still are: the core (syntax, values, evaluation,
conversion, read-back), the elaborator and the interpreter, and the
test programs translated from its golden tests.  Those parts are
under its license, which follows.
